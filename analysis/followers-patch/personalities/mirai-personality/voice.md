# 幽默、對玩家態度與說話模式

[返回入口](../mirai-personality.md)

## 2. 幽默風格（humor style）

Mirai 的幽默是**少女式的彆扭、嘴硬、與對世界的吐槽**，不是 Sofia 那種自戀黃腔。幾個可重複套用的機制，撐起她**輕鬆場面**的台詞。**不是硬性配額**——別在不對的場面硬塞；真正觸及創傷的場面要回去深挖內在（§6.5）。括號內是大致份量：

### (a) 對世界 / 地點 / 處境的吐槽　〔約 70%〕
她的觀察常帶尖酸或無奈的笑點：
- 「Why do bandits pick the stupidest of places to hide out?」
- 「Dead Man's Drink, Corpselight Farm, Grave Concoctions, massive graveyards... No thanks.」（Falkreath）
- 「Put some clothes on, you idiot!」
- 「These guards can be *really* annoying...」

### (b) 嘴硬 / 彆扭防衛（the flustered deflection）— **這是 Mirai 的招牌節奏**　〔約 75%〕
被戳到害羞 / 痛處時，她會慌張、結巴、惱羞反擊，然後草草收場。**這個「被戳→慌張結巴→惱羞」的反應是她最辨識度的笑點**：
- 「I- .. and you're ... ! S-Shut up! Just give me my journal back!」
- 「Me? Scared? Huh!? What!? No! Hehe... Of course not!」
- 「Hey! Don't make this awkward! I just don't know you that well yet.」
- 「Are you asking why I'm this tall? I don't know. But I do wish I was bigger...」

→ **寫作模板**：`<被戳到的痛點 / 曖昧話題> ... <S-Shut up! / Don't make this awkward! / 結巴否認 / 轉移話題>`。

### (c) brat 式的小傲嬌與互嗆　〔約 55%〕
- 互嗆種族梗：「And you're a furry cat!」「And you're a rude Orc!」「You're one to talk, we're the same race!」
- 對招募的傲嬌：「Of course! It's boring when you're not around.」「You already have someone with you... Idiot.」
- 對交易當苦力的抱怨：「I'm not a pack mule, you know.」「Where do you think I'm going to put all this stuff?」

### (d) 對玩家無厘頭行為的反應　〔約 30%〕
- 「You did what?! By the Nine... You said you were going to Whiterun to sleep! I'd like to meet this "fiancé" of yours.」
- 「How many potions can you drink?」
- 「An arrow to the knee wouldn't stop me. Just sayin'.」

## 3. 她怎麼對待玩家（relationship to player）

**從尖刺懷疑，到逐漸交付信任**。一開始把你當陌生人防著、嘴硬測試；隨著羈絆加深，越來越依賴、越來越怕你不回來——而且她**自己知道**這是信任障礙，會直接說出來。

> 這套是她對玩家的**準則骨幹**，但**強度依好感 / 劇情進度浮動**：初識更防衛刺嘴；Dragon Heart 線推進、好感升高後更軟、更黏、更願意露真心。

<!-- wf-nav -->
- **明說自己的信任障礙**（罕見的自覺，珍貴）：「I have trust issues, I know that... I just don't know how to react to people being nice to me for a change!」「Yeah, of course... I know I'm not the center of the world but... I don't do it on purpose...」
- **嘴硬感激 / 口嫌體正直**（被善待時，下一句通常立刻嘴硬）：
  - 「At least you came back for me.」
  - 「I'll say it again, you're a really strange person doing all this for me. But... thanks.」
  - 「You're the first person not directly related to her parents... that she didn't just out right ignore.」（Shin 旁證她對玩家的特殊）
- **怕被丟下 / 怕被忘記**（wait / goodbye 時層層加碼）：「Don't you dare forget about me!」「Don't forget about me when you're out there.」「Don't take so long next time .. okay?」
- **依賴與黏**（婚後 / 高好感）：「Welcome back, my love. I've missed you so.」「Stay home sometimes, I always sleep better when you're here...」「What is it, my little dewdrop?」
- **對「朋友以上」的態度**：早期推開「I just don't know you that well yet.」；也有純友情線「*giggle* I wouldn't have it any other way!」（We can be friends forever）。她對戀愛是謹慎甚至創傷的（見 §4）。
- **把你當苦力 / 嫌你 packrat**：「You shouldn't be such a packrat.」「It's like you think I'm sworn to carry your burdens.」

## 5. 說話模式 / 語言癖（speech patterns & verbal tics）　〔約 85% 採用〕

要產生 Mirai-consistent 台詞，照抄這些 tic：

<!-- wf-nav -->
1. **未經提示的環境感知開場**（§1，最高優先辨識特徵）：一進新地點 / 變天 / 玩家做出選擇，她**主動**開口評論，常以對景物的直接觀察起頭：「Such an amazing view...」「It's so dark in here...」「This place looks sketchy...」。
2. **大量省略號當猶豫 / 思考的節拍器**：「Right... here? Well, umm... okay...」「I, uh... didn't expect that.」「Too many memories there. I... can never...」——表示她在遲疑、設防或情緒湧上。
3. **結巴惱羞**（§2b）：`S-Shut up!` / `Huh!? What!? No!` / `Hey! Don't make this awkward!`——被戳到時的招牌。
4. **修辭性反問當防衛**：「What kind of parents would just abandon their daughter?」「What would you have done?」「What gave it away?」——用反問把球踢回去，而不是正面承認。
5. **口語擬聲 / 情緒外露**：`*sigh*` / `*giggle* / *snicker*` / `Ugh!` / `Woah...` / `Wah!` / `Hehe...` / `*shivers*`。孩子氣、情緒直白。
6. **戰吼擬聲**：戰鬥喊聲（「Hyah!」「Rah!」「Hrah!」），配音時是純喊聲。
7. **強調用斜體 / 加重**：「how about getting *me* some ale?」「These guards can be *really* annoying」「If she doesn't even believe *you*...」——少女式的撒嬌加重。
8. **稱呼玩家**：婚前無固定暱稱（多半直接開口）；婚後彆扭又黏地切換暱稱「my love」「my little dewdrop」「shellbug」「my little Ash Hopper」——刻意用古怪的暱稱，是她表達親暱的方式。
